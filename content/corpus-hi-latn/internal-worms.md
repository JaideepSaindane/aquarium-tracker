Internal worms, sabse commonly *Camallanus* (ek nematode jo visibly distinctive hai — thin, red ya pink worms
jo fish ke vent se protrude karte hain) aur *Capillaria* (usually sirf weight loss aur stringy faeces se infer
kiya jaata hai, directly dekha nahi jaata), ek common lekin frequently mishandled parasite group hain. Kyunki
Camallanus visually alarming hota hai, ye keepers ko panic mein daal kar folk remedies — salt, heat — ki taraf
le jaata hai, jinka gut mein rehne wale internal nematode parasite par koi real effect nahi hota.

Correct treatment ek proper dewormer hai — levamisole ya fenbendazole-based products standard, effective
choices hain — poore tank ke liye dose kiya jaana chahiye, kyunki doosre fish bhi bina visible symptoms ke
already exposed hone ki bahut possibility hoti hai. Ek single treatment akele rarely sufficient hoti hai: in
parasites ke life cycles mein typically substrate ya water mein already present eggs ya larvae shaamil hote
hain jo first dose ke baad bhi hatch hote rahenge, isliye product ke apne instructions ke hisaab se repeat
treatment standard practice hai, overkill nahi.

Fenbendazole specifically flag karne layak hai: ye nematodes ke against broadly effective hai, lekin ye
snails aur kuch doosri invertebrate life ke liye bhi harmful hai, isliye wanted snail populations wale tanks
ko treatment ke dauran ya to ek alag medication choice chahiye ya snails ki careful protection.
