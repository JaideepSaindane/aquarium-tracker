Overfeeding ek quiet, cumulative root cause hai jo bahut saare "meri fish sick hai" reports ke peeche hota hai jinko kabhi isse trace back nahi kiya jaata, kyunki iske effects — elevated ammonia aur nitrate, cloudy water, kabhi-kabhi swim-bladder issues — separate, unrelated problems jaise lagte hain, na ki ek hi habit ke symptoms.

Core misunderstanding yeh hai ki fish ka khaane ke liye visible eagerness, iska reliable signal nahi hai ki unhe actually kitna chahiye. Zyadatar species apni nutritional requirement se kaafi zyada food accept aur beg karengi, toh appetite ke basis par feed karna (fixed, modest portion ke jagah) consistently overshoot karta hai. Practical rule — sirf woh feed kariye jo 2-3 minute mein fully consume ho jaaye, most community species ke liye din mein ek ya do baar — exactly isi appetite aur actual need ke mismatch ko counter karne ke liye hai.

Tank mein bacha hua uneaten food problem ko fish ki apni consumption se aage badhata hai: woh decompose hokar directly ammonia aur nitrate load mein add hota hai, chahe woh eventually khaya jaaye ya nahi — isi liye visible leftover food nikaalna bhi portion control jitna hi important hai. Jab genuinely uncertain ho ki kitna feed karna hai, toh thoda kam ki taraf err karna zyada safe default hai — zyadatar adult fish occasional light ya skipped feeding ko bina real harm ke tolerate kar leti hain.
