Hole-in-the-head disease chhote pits ya eroded areas ke roop mein present hoti hai, typically eyes aur head
ke aas paas start hoti hai aur kabhi kabhi lateral line tak extend hoti hai, sabse classically large cichlids
jaise oscars aur discus mein dekhi jaati hai lekin doosri species mein bhi report hui hai. Ye aksar sirf poor
water quality se hone wale general lateral line erosion se confuse ho jaati hai — aur kabhi kabhi saath mein
bhi hoti hai — yehi ek wajah hai ki exact cause hobby mein genuinely debate ki jaati hai.

Protozoan parasite *Hexamita* commonly implicated hai, lekin hobby ka apna experience aur available evidence
water quality aur nutrition ko kam se kam equally important contributing factors batate hain, aur shayad kayi
cases mein primary drivers — parasite kayi otherwise healthy fish mein low levels par present ho sakta hai
aur tabhi visible problem banta hai jab fish poor water ya lambe samay tak nutritionally poor diet se
stressed ho.

Yehi wajah hai ki correct first response automatically antibiotics ya antiparasitic reach karna nahi hai —
ye water quality aur diet review aur improve karna hai, jo akele hi kayi cases mein progression rok deta hai.
Medication (metronidazole, typically medicated food ke through kyunki parasite internally rehta hai) ek
reasonable next step hai agar water aur diet improve karne se erosion nahi rukta, first move nahi.
