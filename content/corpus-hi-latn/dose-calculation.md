Is corpus ki har medication dose, aur hobby mein generally har product label, tank ke actual water volume ke against calculate ki jaati hai - uske nominal ya box size ke against nahi. Ye distinction plainly stated sunne mein obvious lagta hai, lekin practice mein ye medication overdose ka ek sabse common aur consequential real-world source hai, kyunki zyadatar keepers tank ke box ya listing par likhe size ke hisaab se dose karte hain, us mein actually maujood paani ki true volume ke hisaab se nahi.

Substrate, decor, hardscape, equipment, aur unfilled water line (zyadatar tanks brim-full nahi bhari jaati) sab paani ko displace karte hain, aur combined effect true water volume ko tank ke advertised size ke comparison mein routinely 20-35% kam kar deta hai. Ek medication jo "200 litre tank" ke liye dose ki gayi ho jabki substrate aur decor ke baad actually 140 litres paani ho, effectively intended concentration se roughly 40% zyada dose ho jaati hai - ek meaningful overdose calculation error se, product mein hi kisi galti se nahi.

Practical fix straightforward hai: kuch bhi dose karne se pehle actual dimensions aur fill level se real water volume calculate kariye, substrate aur large decor ke liye neeche adjust karke - aur jab genuinely uncertain hon, ek aisi volume estimate ki taraf err kariye jo true water ko slightly overstate kare, understate nahi, kyunki isse underdosing hoti hai (usually sirf slower ya weaker effect) zyada dangerous overdose direction ki jagah.
