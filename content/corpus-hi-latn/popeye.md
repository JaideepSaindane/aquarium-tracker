Popeye — ek ya dono aankhon ka visible bulging — ek symptom hai jiske do kaafi different likely explanations hote hain, ek single distinguishing detail par depend karte hue: kya yeh ek aankh ya dono aankhon ko affect kar raha hai. Yeh distinction is case ke baare mein almost kisi bhi cheez se zyada matter karta hai, aur yeh pehla sawal poocha jaana chahiye.

Ek single bulging eye (unilateral) zyadatar straightforward physical injury hoti hai — fish ne kahin strike kiya, ya kisi tankmate ne strike kiya — jisse local fluid buildup hota hai. Yeh cases aksar injury site par ek secondary infection rokne ke liye sirf clean water se hi resolve ho jaate hain, aur typically medication ki need nahi hoti.

Dono aankhein bulge karna (bilateral) kisi local injury ke jagah systemic cheez ki taraf point karta hai — ek bacterial infection, poor water quality jo poori fish ko affect kar rahi ho, ya rare cases mein equipment issue se dissolved gas supersaturation. Bilateral popeye ko doosre systemic-illness signs jitna hi concern deserve karta hai, aur dropsy (corpus:dropsy) ke saath ya uske ek early stage ke roop mein bhi dikh sakta hai, isliye aankhon ko isolation mein treat karne ke jagah fish ki overall condition aur tank ki water quality ka ek broader review warranted hai.
