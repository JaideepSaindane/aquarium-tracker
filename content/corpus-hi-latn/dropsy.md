Dropsy khud ek disease nahi hai - ye ek sign hai, almost hamesha serious internal organ failure ka (sabse commonly ek systemic bacterial infection se kidney failure) jisse body cavity mein fluid build up ho jaata hai aur, advanced cases mein, scales bahar ki taraf protrude ho jaati hain us distinctive "pinecone" appearance mein jo condition ko keepers ke beech uska common nickname deti hai.

Ye corpus prognosis par honesty-first position leta hai: jab tak scales visibly protrude hoti hain, significant internal organ damage usually already ho chuki hoti hai, aur uss stage par reported treatment success genuinely low hoti hai hobby literature aur veterinary sources mein. Ye try karna discourage karne ka reason nahi hai - pineconing se pehle early intervention, jab sirf mild swelling present ho, better outlook rakhta hai - lekin visible pineconing ke baare mein poochne wale user ko odds ke baare mein ek honest answer chahiye, false reassurance nahi jo ek difficult lekin kabhi-kabhi kinder decision ko delay kare.

Supportive treatment ek antibacterial medication (likely underlying infection address karta hai) ko Epsom salt bath (magnesium sulfate, regular aquarium salt jaisa nahi) ke saath combine karta hai, jo osmosis ke through fluid retention kam karne mein help karta hai. Jahan koi fish clearly suffer kar rahi ho ek advanced, non-responding case ke saath, humane euthanasia ek legitimate aur kabhi-kabhi sabse responsible option hai, aur ye corpus us conversation ko complete, honest guidance dene ka hissa maanta hai, mention karne se bachne wali cheez nahi.
