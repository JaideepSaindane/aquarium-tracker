Overstocking ek root cause hai jo quietly is corpus ke bahut saare doosre problems ko drive karta hai — nitrate creep, aggression, chronic poor water quality — bina zaroori ek alag, apne aap ka problem dikhe. Yeh specifically Indian retail context mein bhi genuinely common trap hai, jahan point of sale par advice routinely adult size aur real space requirements ko understate karti hai, aur ek chote bowl mein single goldfish ki images "normal" fishkeeping ki widely circulated (aur biologically inappropriate) picture bani rehti hain.

Core mistake yeh hai ki fish ko unke purchase-time size ke basis par khareedna aur stock karna, na ki unke adult size aur real space/social needs ke basis par — bahut saari popular species jo chote size mein bikti hain, substantially bigger grow karti hain, ya unke juvenile size se zyada bade groups ya swimming space ki need hoti hai. Overstocking phir gradually compound hota hai: fish time ke saath incrementally add hote hain, har ek individually reasonable, bina kisi ke tank ka total bioload uski actual capacity ke against recalculate kiye jab stock badhta hai.

Koi single reliable formula nahi hai (popular "inch per gallon" rule ek rough, aksar misleading oversimplification hai) — right approach yeh hai ki har specific species ka adult size, social needs, aur bioload research kiya jaaye khareedne se pehle, aur is research ko actual stocking decision maana jaaye, na ki fish ghar aane ke baad ki afterthought.
