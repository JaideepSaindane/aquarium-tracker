True fungus, jo *Saprolegnia* aur related water moulds se hota hai, genuinely fungal hai — columnaris ke ulat, jo apne "cotton wool" aur "mouth fungus" nicknames ke bawajood bacterial hai. Dono ko correctly alag pehchaanna matter karta hai kyunki inhe poori tarah different medication classes chahiye, aur ek ko doosra maan kar treat karna critical time waste karta hai, especially columnaris kitni fast maar sakta hai yeh dekhte hue.

Sabse clear practical differentiator yeh hai ki growth kis cheez par hai aur kahan start hui. True fungus largely opportunistic hota hai — yeh us tissue par takes hold karta hai jo already damaged, dead, ya otherwise compromised hai (ek existing wound, dead tissue, unfertilised fish eggs) na ki healthy, intact skin par directly attack, aur yeh columnaris se zyada slowly progress karta hai. Columnaris zyada classically mouth ke aas-paas ya back par ek saddle-shaped patch ke roop mein start hota hai aur itna fast progress karta hai ki din mein hi lethal ho sake.

Practically, agar growth clearly ek prior injury se associated hai aur normal, slower pace par progress kar rahi hai, toh appropriate antifungal treatment ke saath true fungus reasonable presumptive diagnosis hai. Agar genuine uncertainty hai, ya growth mouth se start hui ho ya fast move kar rahi ho, toh pehle columnaris ke liye presumptively treat karna safer default hai, dekhte hue ki har condition kitna time deta hai fatal hone se pehle.
