Fin rot sabse commonly reported fish health issues mein se ek hai, aur saath hi sabse commonly mistreated bhi - ye usually ek water-quality symptom hota hai jo disease ka costume pehne hue hai. Fraying, receding fin edges typically ek secondary bacterial (kabhi-kabhi fungal) infection hoti hai jo fin tissue par hold le rahi hai jo already stress se weakened hai, most often elevated ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, ya generally infrequent water changes se.

Visible fin damage ko antibacterial medication se treat karna jabki underlying water quality unaddressed rehti hai, symptom ko treat karta hai cause ko nahi, aur tank ko unnecessary antibiotic use ke real downsides mein expose karta hai - ek damaged biofilter aur encouraged antibiotic resistance - ek aisi problem ke liye jo aksar better water changes se hi resolve ho jaati hai.

Correct sequence hai pehle water quality: correct kariye, change frequency badhaiye, aur kuch dinon tak watch kariye. Mild se moderate fin rot jo iss tarike se address ki jaaye commonly progress karna rokti hai aur fins regrow hone lagti hain. Medication sahi next step hai sirf tabhi jab akele wo progression rokne mein fail ho, ya agar case already advanced ho - fish ke body ki taraf pahunch raha ho, ya fin base par visible redness ya inflammation ke saath ho, jo ek zyada serious infection ki taraf point karta hai jise zyada assertive treatment chahiye.
