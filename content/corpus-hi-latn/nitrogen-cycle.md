Fish waste, bina khaya food aur decaying plant matter toot kar ammonia (NH3/NH4+) banate hain, jo low
concentrations par bhi toxic hai. Bacteria ke do naturally occurring groups tank ke filter media, substrate
aur kisi bhi hard surface ko colonise karte hain, aur ek saath ye do steps mein ise neutralise karte hain.
Pehla group ammonia ko nitrite (NO2-) mein oxidise karta hai, jo bhi toxic hai — ye fish ki apne blood mein
oxygen carry karne ki ability ko interfere karta hai. Doosra group us nitrite ko nitrate (NO3-) mein oxidise
karta hai, jo aquarium concentrations par comparatively low-toxicity hai aur mainly water changes se remove
hota hai (dekhiye corpus:nitrate-creep ye jaanne ke liye ki jab ye nahi hota to kya hota hai).

Yehi hai "the cycle": ammonia → nitrite → nitrate. Ek bilkul naya tank mein ye bacteria useful numbers mein
bilkul nahi hote, aur ek colony build up karna jo tank ke fish load ke saath pace rakh sake, roughly 4-8
hafte leta hai, temperature, ammonia source ki consistency, aur filter bacteria colonise karne ke liye kitna
surface area provide karta hai, iske hisaab se. Ye ek biological process hai jiska apna khud ka timeline hai
— koi product ya technique ise outright skip nahi karti, halaanki kuch (dekhiye corpus:bottled-bacteria-claims,
aur established tank se media ya gravel se seeding karna) ise meaningfully shorten kar sakte hain.

Ye process chalane ke do tarike exist karte hain. Fishless cycling (corpus:fishless-cycling) ek empty tank
mein ek ammonia source dose karti hai aur kisi bhi fish ke andar jaane se pehle poori ammonia→nitrite→nitrate
progression ka wait karti hai — koi livestock kabhi risk mein nahi hota. Fish-in cycling (corpus:fish-in-cycling)
ek small, hardy fish load ke already present hone se start hoti hai aur unke aas paas bacteria establish hote
hue frequent water changes se ammonia/nitrite manage karti hai — riskier hai, lekin yahi hai jahan zyada tar
beginners jinhe pehle se cycling ke baare mein pata nahi tha actually hote hain, aur iska apna damage-limitation
protocol hai, ye lecture dene ke bajaye ki kya hona chahiye tha.

Ek baar established hone ke baad, biofilter ek living population hai jise tank ki zaroorat ke size par bane
rehne ke liye ek continuous, roughly steady ammonia source chahiye — yehi wajah hai ki ye ek lambe unfed period,
ek badi medication course, ya media se takrane wale chlorinated water se crash ho sakta hai (corpus:cycle-crash),
aur isliye ise ek ongoing cheez ke roop mein samajhna worth hai jise protect karna hai, ek one-time task nahi
jise finish karke bhool jaana hai.
