Gill flukes (*Dactylogyrus* aur related monogenean parasites) gill tissue par rehte hain aur usko directly
damage karte hain, aur ich ya velvet ke unlike, ye essentially microscope ke bina invisible hote hain — koi
external spot ya coating nahi hoti jise dekha ja sake. Isi wajah se ye laboured breathing ke sabse commonly
misdiagnosed causes mein se ek hote hain: koi keeper ammonia, nitrite, nitrate aur temperature test karta hai,
sab normal aata hai, aur wo maan leta hai ki fish theek hai — jabki actual cause ek parasite hai jo kabhi bhi
water test mein nahi dikhega.

Practical diagnostic pattern exactly yehi combination hai — tez ya laboured breathing, clamped ya flared
gills, flashing, aur genuinely normal water parameters ke saath. Kyunki home aquarists realistically gill
scrape aur microscope examination nahi kar sakte, is symptom pattern ke basis par presumptive treatment hi
standard, accepted approach hai, definitive confirmation ka wait karne ke bajaye.

Treatment ke liye ek medication chahiye jo specifically monogenean flukes ke against effective ho —
praziquantel-based products standard choice hain — generic broad-spectrum treatment ki jagah, jinme se kayi
is parasite class ke against simply kaam nahi karti. Kuch din baad ek repeat dose typically zaroori hoti hai
un eggs ko catch karne ke liye jo first treatment ke baad survive kar ke hatch ho jaate hain.
