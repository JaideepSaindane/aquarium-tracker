Columnaris (*Flavobacterium columnare*) common fish diseases mein sabse fast-killing mein se ek hai, jo pehle visible signs se death tak 24-72 hours mein progress kar sakta hai - ich se faster, aur urgency mein velvet ke comparable. Iski classic "cotton wool" ya "mouth fungus" appearance ke bawajood ye bacterial hai, jo iska sabse consequential misdiagnosis trap bhi hai: cottony white patches fungal lagte hain, jisse keepers ek antifungal treatment try karte hain jo actual bacterial cause ke against kuch nahi karta, jabki disease is corpus mein possible sabse fastest timeline par progress karti rehti hai.

Doosra critical safety point temperature hai. Hobby mein kai doosre common treatments tank temperature badhane par lean karte hain - jaise ich ke vulnerable free-swimming stage ko ye speed up karta hai - aur ye specifically columnaris ke saath ek dangerous, easy-to-make mistake create karta hai: heat badhana bacteria ki apni progression ko accelerate karta hai, uske against fight karne mein help karne ki jagah. Standard "disease treat karne ke liye tank warm karo" reflex yahan actively harmful hai, aur ye single sabse important cheez hai jo columnaris suspect hone par right karni chahiye.

Iss disease ki speed dekhte hue, treatment shuru ho jaana chahiye jaise hi suspect ho - correct identification (bacterial, fungal nahi, cotton-wool jaise patches, aksar mooh se start hote hain ya saddle-shaped patch ke roop mein) aur promptly antibacterial treatment fish ko best chance dete hain, aur "wait and see" approach ke liye bahut kam margin hai jo shayad kisi slower-moving condition ke liye reasonable ho.
