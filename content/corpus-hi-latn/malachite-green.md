Malachite green ich aur kayi doosre external parasites ke against ek effective, well-established treatment
hai, jo frequently commercial "ich treatment" products mein formalin ke combination mein becha jaata hai. Ye
do genuine, well-supported cautions carry karta hai: ye teratogenic hai, matlab ye developing fish eggs ko
harm kar sakta hai, isliye eggs present wale active breeding setup mein isko kabhi use nahi karna chahiye, aur
ye silicone aur decor ko stain kar sakta hai.

Hobby mein kabhi kabhi repeat hone wala ek blanket claim — ki malachite green sab scaleless fish ya sab
invertebrates ke liye categorically unsafe hai — evidence se well supported nahi hai aur ise directly correct
karna worth hai, kyunki isne kuch keepers ko un alternatives (aquarium salt, copper-based treatments) ki taraf
push kiya hai jo exact un species (kayi scaleless fish, zyada tar invertebrates) ke liye actually zyada
dangerous hain jinhe avoidance protect karna chahti thi. Responsible position ye hai ki strictly label
instructions ke hisaab se dose kiya jaaye aur specific product aur species combination check kiya jaaye,
medication category ko poori tarah avoid na kiya jaaye ek overly broad rule ke basis par.
