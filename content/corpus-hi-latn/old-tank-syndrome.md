symptoms:
  - Fish jo lambe time se (bina regular maintenance wale tank mein) theek-thaak ya halka sa unwell lag rahi thi
  - Ek "proper" water change ya cleanup ke thodi der baad, bahut neglect kiye gaye tank mein fish mar jaana ya acute distress dikhana
  - Old-tank-syndrome wale tank ko test karne par usually bahut low pH, high nitrate, aur high dissolved mineral content milta hai
  - Fish jo normally dangerous samjhe jaane wale parameters ko ajeeb tarah se tolerate karti dikhein
likely_causes:
  - Tank jisme lambe time se (aksar kayi mahine ya saal) se water change bilkul nahi ya bahut kam hua ho
  - Fish dheere-dheere kharaab ho rahi water quality ke saath adapt ho jaati hain — acidic pH, high nitrate, high dissolved solids — kyunki change itna slow tha ki body physiologically adapt kar sakti thi
  - Ek well-meaning "chalo isko ab proper clean kar dete hain" wala bada water change ya full substrate clean, jo suddenly parameters ko un fish ke adapted level se bahut different reading par le aata hai
immediate_actions:
  - Kuch bhi karne se pehle current parameters fully test kariye (pH, ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, KH, GH) — aapko yeh baseline chahiye ki fish actually kis level par adapted hain, na ki parameters "kya hone chahiye".
  - Agar already bada change ya cleanup ho gaya hai aur fish distress dikha rahi hain, toh isko kisi bhi acute parameter swing jaisa treat kariye: aeration badhaiye, closely observe kariye, aur dobara bada change karke "fix" karne ki koshish bilkul mat kariye — isse shock aur badh jaata hai.
  - Agar abhi tak bada change nahi kiya hai — toh mat kariye. Iske jagah din-din ke gap se chote water changes (10-15%) kariye, har change ke beech re-test kariye, aur tank ko normal parameters par weeks mein wapas laane ka plan banaiye, ek session mein nahi.
  - Filter media ko nikale hue tank water mein gently rinse/clean kariye, ek saath sab replace mat kariye — isse parameter shock ke saath biofilter crash bhi nahi hoga.
  - Agar substrate kabhi clean nahi hua, toh usse ek saath disturb karne ke jagah multiple sessions mein gradually clean kariye — ek saath karne se bahut trapped waste ek saath water column mein release ho sakta hai.
do_not_do:
  - Lambe time se neglect kiye gaye tank par pehla step ke roop mein ek single bada water change (50%+) mat kariye. Poor water ke physiologically adapted fish, jab parameters fast "normal" ki taraf move hote hain, fatal osmotic aur pH shock mein ja sakti hain.
  - Ek tank jiska substrate kabhi clean nahi hua, uska full vacuum/clean ek session mein mat kariye — isse trapped ammonia aur organic waste ka bada pulse ek saath release ho sakta hai.
  - Bade water change ke saath sab filter media ek saath replace mat kariye — biofilter khona aur parameter swing dono ek saath hona sabse worst-case combination hai.
  - Yeh mat sochiye ki neglected tank mein fish "theek dikh rahi hain" matlab tank actually theek hai — chronic adaptation ek real underlying problem ko chhupa deta hai jo sudden change se expose ho jaata hai.
when_to_escalate:
  - Change ho jaane ke baad multiple fish distress dikha rahi hain — isse careful, slow stabilisation chahiye, aur intervention nahi; recovery ki pacing ke liye kisi experienced keeper ki guidance leejiye.
  - Tank ki history genuinely unknown ho (jaise inherited ya bahut lambe time se unattended tank) — by default isko old tank syndrome maan kar slow chaliye, chahe fish abhi theek dikh rahi ho.

Old tank syndrome tab hota hai jab ek tank lambe time tak — aksar mahino tak — proper maintenance ke bina rehta hai, aur uske andar ki fish dheere-dheere, physiologically, steadily kharaab ho rahi water ke saath adapt ho jaati hain: falling pH, badhta nitrate, badhta dissolved solids. Kyunki yeh change gradually hota hai, fish ki body ko compensate karne ka time mil jaata hai, aur woh ek aisi water mein bhi outwardly normal dikh sakti hain jo suddenly aati toh immediately dangerous hoti.

Danger neglect khud nahi hai — danger woh well-intentioned fix hai. Ek keeper jise pata chalta hai ki tank neglect hua hai, guilty feel karta hai, aur ek single bada "chalo ab isko proper sort karte hain" water change ya deep clean kar deta hai — woh fish ke environment ko unki adapted physiology se zyada fast "normal" ki taraf move kar raha hota hai. Jo osmotic aur pH shock result hota hai, wahi aksar un fish ko maar deta hai jo neglect ko quietly survive kar rahi thi.

Correct response natural instinct ko invert karta hai: slow jaao. Tank ko healthy parameters par days se weeks mein laaiye, chote-chote staged water changes aur gradual substrate cleaning se, har step ke beech test kariye — "isko normal par laana" ko ek urgent, single cleanup session ki tarah treat mat kariye.
