likely_causes:
  - Heater ke andar ek failed thermostat, jo actual water temperature ki parwaah kiye bina "on" position mein stuck hai
  - Ek cheap ya purana heater jiska koi reliable cutoff nahi hai, older ya low-cost units mein common
  - Ek heater jo fully submerged nahi hai ya still-water zone ke paas position kiya gaya hai, jisse wo surrounding temperature misread kar raha hai aur heat karta rehta hai
immediate_actions:
  - Heater ko immediately unplug kariye — ye sabse urgent step hai aur sab kuch aur se pehle aata hai.
  - Water ko ek separate, independent thermometer se test kariye, actual temperature confirm karne ke liye, heater ke apne display se nahi.
  - Agar water significantly overheated hai, to cooler dechlorinated water se ek partial water change kariye taaki temperature gradually kam ho, ek large drop mein nahi.
  - Surface agitation/aeration immediately badha dijiye — garam paani mein dissolved oxygen kam hota hai, jo heat se already stressed fish ke liye crisis ko compound karta hai.
  - Temperature stabilise hone ke baad, heater ko poori tarah replace kariye — jo heater stuck on hua ho use reuse mat kariye, chahe wo unplug aur cool hone ke baad "theek" bhi lage. Species check ke liye ise kabhi wapas plug in mat kariye.
do_not_do:
  - Overheated tank ko ek large, fast temperature drop se cool mat kariye — sudden swings khud stressor hote hain; partial water changes se temperature gradually kam kariye.
  - Jo heater ek baar stuck on ho gaya ho use reuse mat kariye, chahe wo unplug ke baad tank ke bahar retest karne par phir se kaam karta lage — ek thermostat jo already ek baar fail ho chuka hai unreliable hai.
  - Actual tank temperature judge karne ke liye sirf heater ke apne built-in display/dial par rely mat kariye — hamesha ek independent thermometer se confirm kariye.

Heater stuck on hona ek fast-moving emergency hai kyunki garam paani progressively kam dissolved oxygen hold
karta hai, aur usi waqt fish ka metabolic demand heat ke saath badhta jaata hai — dono effects ek dusre ko
jaldi compound karte hain. Sabse pehla aur sabse urgent action hamesha heater ko unplug karna hai, kuch aur
karne se pehle, fish ko check karne se bhi pehle.

Independent verification yahan specifically matter karta hai kyunki failed thermostat wala heater apna
temperature accurately report nahi kar sakta — ek separate thermometer hi reliable tarika hai yeh jaanne ka
ki situation actually kitni bad hai, aur ye confirm karne ka ki water genuinely ek safe range tak cool ho
chuka hai, emergency ko over consider karne se pehle.

Cooling still gradual honi chahiye. Ek overheated tank already ek stressed system hai, aur temperature ko
bahut jaldi ek bahut bade, bahut cold water change se neeche laana pehle wale shock ke upar dusra shock daal
deta hai. Cooler (cold nahi) dechlorinated water se partial water changes temperature ko ek slightly longer
window mein safely kam karte hain. Stable hone ke baad, heater ko khud retire kar dena chahiye — ek
thermostat jo ek baar fail ho chuka hai, usne demonstrate kar diya hai ki wo trust nahi kiya ja sakta, baad
mein kaise bhi test kare.
