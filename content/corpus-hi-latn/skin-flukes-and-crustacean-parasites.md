Yeh group protozoa se bade visible external parasites cover karta hai: anchor worm (*Lernaea*, ek thread-like crustacean jo burrow in karta hai aur visible threads trail karta hai), fish lice (*Argulus*, ek flat disc-shaped crustacean jo skin par baithta hai), aur skin flukes (*Gyrodactylus*, generally individually dikhne ke liye bahut chota lekin flashing, excess mucus aur patchy skin cause karta hai).

Sabse common mistake ek visible attached parasite ko ek simple mechanical problem maan kar treat karna hai — pull it off, done. Practice mein, visible worm ya louse ek life cycle ka sirf adult stage hai jiske free-swimming ya egg stages already tank ke paani mein kahin aur maujood hain, isliye tank ko medicate kiye bina sirf ek visible parasite ko remove karna infestation ko intact chhod deta hai aur aksar embedded mouthparts bhi chhod deta hai jo ek infection site ban jaate hain.

Treatment ko actual parasite group ke saath match karna zaroori hai — skin flukes ke liye praziquantel-based products, aur anchor worm aur fish lice ke liye specifically crustacean parasites ke liye labelled ek medication, kyunki yeh biologically different parasite classes hain aur ek ke against effective treatment doosre ke against reliably kaam nahi karta. Zyadatar external parasites ki tarah, kuch din se ek week baad ek repeat treatment typically zaroori hota hai un newly hatched individuals ko catch karne ke liye jinhe first round miss kar gaya.
