Zyadatar municipal water supplies ya to free chlorine ya chloramine (chlorine aur ammonia ka combination, jise bahut se utilities isliye pasand karte hain kyunki ye distribution pipes mein zyada stable hota hai aur zyada der tak lasts) se treat kiye jaate hain. Dono human drinking-water safety ke liye use hone wale concentrations par fish ke liye toxic hote hain - ye directly gill tissue damage karte hain, aur poisoning water change ke dauraan exposure ke minutes ke andar dikh sakti hai.

Sabse critical distinction jo zyadatar keepers miss karte hain wo ye hai ki in do treatment types ko alag handling chahiye. Plain chlorine ek-do din mein agitation ke saath standing water se off-gas ho jaata hai, aur koi bhi basic dechlorinator ise neutralise kar deta hai. Chloramine dono mein se koi nahi karta - ye baahar rakhne se meaningfully evaporate nahi hota, aur chlorine-only neutraliser chlorine component ko strip kar dega jabki free ammonia peeche chhod dega, jo apne aap mein ek poisoning risk hai. Ek dechlorinator jo explicitly chloramine handle karta ho (zyadatar modern multi-purpose conditioners karte hain, lekin sabhi nahi) hi sirf reliable fix hai, aur ise tank ke true water volume ke hisaab se dose karna zaroori hai, uske nominal size ke hisaab se nahi.

Kyunki local water authority bina public notice ke treatment methods change kar sakta hai, ek routine jo saalon se kaam kar raha tha achanak inadequate ho sakta hai - water changes ke baad repeated unexplained poisoning ko current dechlorinator label aur current local water treatment method ke against check karna worth hai, sirf same tarike se re-dose karne ki jagah.
