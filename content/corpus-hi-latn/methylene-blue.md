Methylene blue ek narrow set of purposes ke liye genuinely ek useful medication hai — sabse zyada fungal-
infected fish eggs treat karne ke liye taaki paas ke viable eggs protect ho sakein, aur ek hospital-tank ya
dip treatment ke roop mein early fungal issues ya certain protozoal support ke liye — lekin ye established
biological filter wale display tank mein use ke liye ek poor fit hai, kyunki ye nitrifying bacteria ko harm
ya strip karne ke liye jaana jaata hai. Wahan ise use karna ek fungal problem ko ammonia problem mein convert
karne ka risk rakhta hai.

Ye jo bhi touch karta hai use bhi stain karta hai — silicone seals, plastic decor, aur plant tissue — aksar
porous surfaces par permanently, jo ek purely cosmetic lekin genuinely irreversible consequence hai jise use
karne se pehle jaanna zaroori hai, especially kisi bhi aise tank mein jiski appearance ki keeper ko parwaah
ho.

Practical rule: methylene blue ek dedicated hospital tank mein ya ek short dip/egg treatment ke roop mein
belong karta hai, ek planted ya biologically mature display tank mein general-purpose ya precautionary dose
ke roop mein nahi.
