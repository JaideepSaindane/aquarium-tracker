Multiple medications stack karna — same tank mein ek saath do ya zyada treatments dose karna, chahe kayi
possible diagnoses ek saath cover karne ke liye ho ya ek pehli treatment ke saath impatience ki wajah se jisne
abhi tak results nahi dikhaye — practically single sabse common tarika hai jisse koi keeper poore tank ko
bachaane ke bajaye accidentally maar deta hai.

Do mechanisms ise itna dangerous banate hain. Pehla, medication interactions kisi bhi specific combination ke
liye aksar untested aur undocumented hote hain — ek product apni labelled dose par individually safe hona ye
kuch nahi batata ki jab ise same water mein ek doosre, different chemical ke saath combine kiya jaaye to kya
hota hai. Doosra, aur practically aksar zyada immediately lethal factor, cumulative oxygen demand hai:
saath mein kayi medications, especially koi bhi jo gill tissue ya metabolic rate ko affect karti hain,
commonly usse zyada hoti hain jo kisi individual product ki label safety testing account karti hai, aur fish
aksar combined oxygen stress se marte hain, ek specific toxic interaction se nahi.

Correct approach disciplined aur slower hai jitna stacking feel karta hai ki hona chahiye: ek likely problem
identify kariye, ise ek appropriate medication se poore intended course ke liye treat kariye, aur sirf tabhi
ek different treatment ki taraf move kariye jab wo course genuinely khatam ho chuka ho ya clearly sahi
diagnosis na ho — sirf isliye nahi kyunki kuch din guzar gaye hain bina koi visible change ke.
