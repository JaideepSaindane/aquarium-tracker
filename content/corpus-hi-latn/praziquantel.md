Praziquantel jo actually treat karta hai uske liye ek genuinely safe aur effective medication hai — monogenean flukes (gill aur skin flukes dono) aur kuch tapeworm infections — jiska margin of safety is corpus ki zyadatar doosri medications se wider hai. Yeh safety aur effectiveness, somewhat ironically, isse ek general "parasite treatment" ke roop mein over-apply karne ki wajah bani hai un conditions ke liye jinhe yeh actually touch hi nahi karta.

Praziquantel protozoan parasites jaise ich ya velvet ke against, nematodes/roundworms (jinhe levamisole ya fenbendazole chahiye, dekhiye corpus:levamisole-fenbendazole) ke against, ya crustacean parasites jaise anchor worm aur fish lice (jinhe crustacean-specific treatment chahiye, dekhiye corpus:skin-flukes-and-crustacean-parasites) ke against kuch meaningful nahi karta. Galat parasite class ke liye isko use karne se koi visible side effect nahi hota — kyunki yeh safe hai — lekin koi result bhi nahi hota, jo ek aisi condition par critical treatment time waste kar sakta hai jahan waiting matter karti hai.

Correct use pattern ek initial dose ke baad roughly 5-7 din baad ek repeat treatment hai, taaki parasite eggs ya newly hatched individuals jinhe first dose reach nahi kar payi unhe catch kiya ja sake — akela ek treatment aksar parasite ki life cycle ko intact chhod deta hai.
