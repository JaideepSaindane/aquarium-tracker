Tank temperature badhana kuch disease treatment protocols ka ek real, supported part hai — sabse zyada ich ke
liye, jahan garam paani parasite ko uske vulnerable free-swimming stage se jaldi guzar deta hai, jisse wo
window chhota ho jaata hai jisme treatment usko actually maar sakta hai. Ye corpus explicitly batata hai ki
hobby aur available sources is technique ke precise optimal protocols par genuinely disagree karte hain,
isliye ise settled nahi, contested mark kiya gaya hai.

Jo contested nahi hai, aur safety ke liye zyada important hai, wo ye ki yehi technique wrong diagnosis par
misapply hone par actively harmful ho jaati hai. Columnaris — ek fast-killing bacterial infection jo kabhi
kabhi doosri conditions se confuse ho jaata hai — higher temperatures par faster progress karta hai, matlab
"disease treat karne ke liye heat badhao" wala instinct jo ich ke liye correct hai, wo actively dangerous ho
jaata hai agar actual problem columnaris hai (dekhiye corpus:columnaris). Is technique tak pahunchne se pehle
diagnosis confirm karna formality nahi hai; ye decide karta hai ki intervention help karega ya disease ko
actively accelerate karega.

Heat treatment ka ek universal cost bhi hota hai, diagnosis kuch bhi ho: garam paani mein dissolved oxygen kam
hota jaata hai, jabki har fish ka metabolic aur oxygen demand heat ke saath badhta hai, isliye ise hamesha
increased aeration ke saath pair kariye aur cool-water species par kabhi use mat kariye jinke liye target
temperature khud hi harmful hai, treat ki ja rahi disease se independent.
