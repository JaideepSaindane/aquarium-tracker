Formalin (ek formaldehyde solution) kai external parasites ke against ek effective treatment hai, aksar commercial ich aur parasite treatments mein malachite green ke saath combine kiya jaata hai, lekin ye do serious risks carry karta hai jo ise is corpus ki zyadatar doosri medications se alag karte hain: ye dissolved oxygen significantly consume karta hai, un fish mein respiratory stress compound karte hue jo already saans lene ke liye struggle kar rahi ho sakti hain, aur ye handling ke dauraan vapour inhalation aur skin/eye contact ke through ek genuine human health hazard hai.

Oxygen-depletion effect sabse zyada matter karta hai ek aisi tank mein jahan fish already gasping ya stressed hain - exactly usi moment par oxygen aur kam karne wala treatment add karna khud apne aap mein real harm kar sakta hai, medication ke parasite par intended effect se independent. Treatment ke dauraan aur baad mein heavy aeration yahan optional supportive care nahi hai; ye is medication ko safely use karne ka ek necessary hissa hai.

Human-health dimension ko equal weight deni chahiye, particularly relevant given ki India mein kitni aquariums chhoti, less-ventilated rooms mein rakhi jaati hain: formaldehyde vapour ek genuine respiratory irritant aur hazard hai, aur formalin ko kisi bhi hazardous household chemical jaisi hi care se handle karna chahiye - good ventilation, avoided skin/eye contact, aur careful, deliberate dosing, "thoda extra sure hone ke liye" ki jagah.
