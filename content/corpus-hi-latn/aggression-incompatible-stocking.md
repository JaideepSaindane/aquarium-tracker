Aggression aur incompatible stocking ek commonly underdiagnosed cause hai injury aur death ka, jise disease se attribute kar diya jaata hai, especially jab symptom torn ya ragged fins ho - ek presentation jo fin rot (corpus:fin-rot) jaisa dikhta hai lekin bilkul alag cause aur bilkul alag fix ki zaroorat hoti hai. Water quality correction aur medication kuch nahi karte ek us injury ke liye jo koi tankmate actively cause kar raha hai.

Ek specific, bahut common aur bahut fixable cause par particular attention deni chahiye: ek schooling species ko uske recommended minimum group size se kam rakhna. Bahut se schooling fish security ke sense ke liye group par hi rely karte hain, aur jab kam number mein rakhe jaate hain, toh resulting social stress aksar doosre tankmates ya khud under-sized group ke andar hi aggression ke roop mein redirect ho jaata hai - school ko proper size tak badhana, stressed fish ko hatane ki jagah, aksar actual fix hota hai.

Broadly, aggression diagnose karne ka matlab hai tank ko directly observe karna - kaun kisko chase kar raha hai, kitni persistently, aur ye one-directional bullying hai ya normal, tolerable social hierarchy behaviour - na ki assume kar lena ki koi bhi injury disease-related hi hogi. Stocking correct karna (group size, species compatibility, adequate territory aur hiding spots) root cause ko address karta hai jaise koi medication nahi kar sakti.
