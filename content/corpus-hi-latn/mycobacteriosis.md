Mycobacteriosis — jise aksar fish TB kaha jaata hai — is poore corpus mein woh ek entry hai jisme genuine
human-health dimension hai, aur ye fact plainly surface hona chahiye, ek footnote ki tarah treat nahi hona
chahiye. Causative *Mycobacterium* species, rare cases mein, broken skin aur contaminated aquarium water ke
beech contact se humans ko infect kar sakta hai, sabse aksar ek localised skin infection produce karta hai
jise kabhi kabhi "fish handler's disease" ya "fish tank granuloma" kaha jaata hai. Gloves ke saath normally
tank water handle karne wale ek healthy person ke liye risk low hai, lekin ye real hai, medical literature
mein well documented hai, aur ek vague mention ke bajaye ek plain, direct warning worth hai.

Fish ke liye khud, ye ek chronic, slow-progressing, aur realistically untreatable condition hai. Ye gradual
wasting ke roop mein present hoti hai — normal appetite ke bawajood weight loss — kabhi kabhi advanced cases
mein spinal curvature ya skin lesions ke saath, weeks se months mein develop hoti hai, acute illness ki tarah
present nahi hoti. Definitive diagnosis ke liye lab testing chahiye jo zyada tar home keepers ko available
nahi hai, isliye ye usually pehle chronic wasting ke doosre, zyada treatable causes ko rule out karke reach
ki gayi ek presumptive diagnosis hoti hai.

Yahan honesty sabse zyada matter karti hai: koi dependable, established cure nahi hai. Literature mein
antibiotic treatment attempts exist karte hain, lekin success rates itne inconsistent hain ki ek worried
keeper ko cure promise karna misleading hoga. Responsible guidance humane management hai — affected fish ko
isolate karna, kisi bhi handling ke dauran human health protect karna, aur fish ki welfare consider karna,
including advanced cases mein euthanasia, ek indefinite aur unlikely-to-succeed treatment attempt se zyada.
