applies_to:
  water_type: [freshwater]

symptoms:
  - Fins aur body par cheeni/suji ke daane jaise white spots, raised, fuzzy nahi
  - Flashing — gravel, rocks, heater, filter intake se rubbing
  - Clamped fins, hiding, kam appetite
  - Rapid gill movement ya surface par gasping (gill infection — worse prognosis)
  - Spots jo ek din ke liye gayab ho jaate hain aur zyada number mein wapas aa jaate hain (parasite reproduce karne ke liye drop off hua hai)
likely_causes:
  - Naye add kiye gaye fish ya plants bina quarantine ke introduce kiye gaye hain (sabse common)
  - Temperature drop ya swing se immunity suppress hona (power cut, winter, bada cold water change)
  - Poor water quality, overstocking ya bullying se chronic stress
  - Infected tank aur clean tank ke beech shared net/siphon
immediate_actions:
  - Kuch bhi dose karne se PEHLE ammonia, nitrite, nitrate aur temperature test kariye. Ich stressed fish par thrive karta hai; agar ammonia ya nitrite 0 se upar hai to aap do problems treat kar rahe hain, aur water zyada urgent hai.
  - Poore tank ko treat kariye, sirf spot wale fish ko nahi. Jab tak spots visible hote hain, tab tak free-swimming stage poore water column mein phel chuka hota hai.
  - Activated carbon aur koi bhi chemical filtration media hata dijiye. Carbon medication ko strip kar deta hai aur dose ko meaningless bana deta hai. Biological media ko na chheden.
  - Aeration badha dijiye — ek airstone add kariye ya outflow neeche kariye taaki surface break ho. Har ich treatment (heat, salt, malachite green, formalin) available oxygen kam karta hai.
  - Neeche diye options mein se EK treatment choose kariye aur poora course complete kariye. Course ke beech mein product switch mat kariye.
  - Garam paani mein 24-48 ghante ke intervals par kam se kam 3 doses ke liye treatment continue kariye, aur poore 7 din ke liye ya last visible spot ke 3 din baad tak, jo bhi baad mein ho.
do_not_do:
  - Spots gayab hone par band mat kariye. Sirf free-swimming tomite stage hi chemicals se marta hai; fish par encysted stage aur substrate mein reproductive cyst untouched rehte hain. Jaldi rokna sabse common cause hai "resistant" second outbreak ka.
  - Ek saath do medications dose mat kariye, ya medication plus salt plus bada heat raise. Cumulative oxygen demand aur liver load parasite se zyada fish maar dete hain.
  - Agar tank mein Corydoras ya doosre chhote catfish, zyada tar loaches (kuhli, yoyo, clown), electroreceptive fish (elephant nose, black ghost knife), soft-water tetras, live plants, shrimp ya snails hain to aquarium salt use mat kariye. Salt tolerance species ke hisaab se sharply vary karta hai aur ye groups intolerant end par hain.
  - Cool-water species — goldfish, White Cloud Mountain minnows, hillstream loaches — ke liye, ya kisi bhi already gasping fish ke liye temperature mat badhaiye. Heat dissolved oxygen exactly usi waqt kam karta hai jab gill-infected fish ko usse zyada zaroorat hoti hai.
  - Agar aapko ich ki jagah columnaris ka suspicion hai (white/grey fuzzy patches, saddle lesions, rapid deterioration), to temperature bilkul mat badhaiye. Columnaris heat ke saath accelerate hota hai aur tank ko 48 ghanton mein maar sakta hai.
  - Shrimp, snail ya kisi bhi doosre invertebrate wale tank mein copper-based medication use mat kariye — ye therapeutic dose par unke liye lethal hai, aur substrate se bind ho kar mahino baad tak leach hota hai.
  - Sensitive fish par "gentle rehne ke liye" dose half mat kariye. Ek sub-therapeutic dose parasite ko nahi maarti aur exposure lamba kar deti hai. Agar koi fish full dose tolerate nahi kar sakta, to alag treatment choose kariye.
  - Fish se spots scrape ya wipe mat kariye. Cysts epidermis ke neeche hote hain; scraping se open wounds aur secondary bacterial infection ho jaata hai.
  - Active outbreak ke dauran UV ya "ich-eating" additives ko sole treatment ke taur par rely mat kariye.
when_to_escalate:
  - Fish surface par gasping kar raha hai lekin body par bahut kam ya koi visible spots nahi — likely gill infestation, poor prognosis, faster intervention aur maximum aeration ki zaroorat hai.
  - Correctly dosed treatment ke 5 din baad bhi koi improvement nahi — diagnosis probably galat hai (velvet, Costia, Chilodonella sab similar dikhte hain lekin same parasite nahi hain).
  - Marine tank — ye entry apply nahi hoti. Marine white spot Cryptocaryon irritans hai, ek different organism jiska different life cycle aur different treatment hai.
  - Water parameters clean confirm hone aur treatment schedule par hone ke bawajood deaths continue ho rahe hain.
treatments:
  - name: Malachite green + formalin combination (e.g. Ich-X, Rid-Ich+)
    dose: Per manufacturer label — commonly 5 ml per 10 US gallons (~38 L). Calculate on actual water volume, not tank rating.
    duration: Every 24 h after a one-third water change, until spots are gone, then one further dose.
    dangerous_to: [fish_eggs, some_inverts_check_label, silicone_and_decor_staining]
    notes: Public aquaria mein widely used hai. Scaleless fish, shrimp aur snails ke liye, jo claim hai wo TOLERANCE ke baare mein hai, efficacy ke baare mein nahi — dekhiye docs/05-content-guide.md §6 mein malachite green ruling. Dose karne se pehle specific product verify kariye, kyunki formalin content brands ke beech vary karta hai. Dissolved oxygen kam karta hai; aerate kariye. Formalin ek human respiratory aur carcinogenic hazard hai — ventilated room mein dose kariye, kabhi bhi closed bathroom mein nahi.
    source: https://www.aquariumcoop.com/blogs/aquarium/ich-treatment
  - name: Formalin (long-term bath, hospital tank)
    dose: 15 mg/L long-term immersion, or 250 mg/L short bath for 30–60 minutes
    duration: Repeat per life-cycle interval; minimum 3 treatments 2–3 days apart at 24–26 °C, 5 treatments 3–5 days apart at ~15 °C
    dangerous_to: [biofilter, low_oxygen_tanks, humans_inhalation]
    notes: Biofilter ke saath use nahi kiya ja sakta — sirf hospital tank mein. Oxygen heavily consume karta hai.
    source: https://extension.rwfm.tamu.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/8/2013/09/Ichthyophthirius-multifiliis-White-Spot-Infections-in-Fish.pdf
  - name: Sodium chloride (aquarium/non-iodised salt), prolonged immersion
    dose: 0.05–0.2% (500–2,000 mg/L) prolonged immersion in a recirculating system. Some sources use up to 0.5% for tolerant species only.
    duration: Maintained through the outbreak, removed by successive water changes afterwards
    dangerous_to: [corydoras, loaches, kuhli_loach, most_tetras, electroreceptive_fish, live_plants, shrimp, snails]
    notes: CONTESTED. Extension aquaculture sources in concentrations par salt ko ek valid ich control list karte hain; doosre reviewers un trials ko cite karte hain jisme 0.25% salt ka ich par koi measurable effect nahi tha aur argue karte hain ki sirf 0.5-0.75% hi kuch karta hai, jo kayi community fish ki tolerance se upar hai. Salt ko tolerant species (goldfish, zyada tar cichlids, livebearers) ke liye ek supportive measure maaniye, reliable cure nahi. UF/IFAS specifically note karta hai ki tetras aur electroreceptive fish jaise elephant nose salt tolerate nahi karte.
    source: https://ask.ifas.ufl.edu/publication/VM007/pdf
  - name: Temperature elevation
    dose: Raise to 28–30 °C (86 °F) at no more than 1–2 °C per day, only if every inhabitant tolerates it
    duration: Held for the duration of chemical treatment
    dangerous_to: [goldfish, white_cloud_minnow, hillstream_loach, cool_water_species, any_gasping_fish, columnaris_cases]
    notes: "CONTESTED. Practical Fishkeeping aur standard hobby practice life cycle accelerate karne ke liye ~30°C tak badhane ki recommend karte hain taaki vulnerable free-swimming stage jaldi medication ke expose ho. Extension guidance pet fish ke liye ek cycling 32°C/21°C protocol describe karta hai. Doosre reviewers batate hain ki controlled studies dikhati hain ki akele heat ineffective hai. AquaAI position: heat ek ADJUNCT hai jo treatment window shorten karta hai, kabhi standalone cure nahi, aur cool-water species aur gill-compromised fish ke liye contraindicated hai."
    source: https://www.practicalfishkeeping.co.uk/features/how-to-solve-a-problem-like-whitespot/
  - name: Copper sulfate
    dose: Alkalinity-dependent; total alkalinity ÷ 100 = mg/L dose. Not usable below 50 mg/L alkalinity.
    duration: Every other day at 24–26 °C; every 4–5 days at ~15 °C
    dangerous_to: [all_invertebrates, shrimp, snails, soft_water_tanks_gh_under_4, plants]
    notes: Pond/aquaculture treatment hai. Planted ya invertebrate display tanks ke liye recommend nahi kiya jaata. Soft water mein therapeutic aur lethal ke beech margin narrow hai.
    source: https://extension.rwfm.tamu.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/8/2013/09/Ichthyophthirius-multifiliis-White-Spot-Infections-in-Fish.pdf

Ich ek ciliate parasite hai jiska three-stage life cycle hota hai, aur inme se sirf ek stage hi maara ja sakta
hai. Trophont fish ki skin ke neeche embedded hota hai — yehi wo white spot hai jo aap dekhte hain, aur ye
wahan protected hota hai. Ye drop off ho kar tomont ban jaata hai, aur substrate ya decor par encyst ho jaata
hai, jahan ye sainkdo free-swimming tomites mein divide ho jaata hai. Sirf tomites hi chemical treatment ke
liye susceptible hote hain, aur unke paas 24-26°C par host dhoondne ke liye roughly 48 ghante hote hain, warna
wo mar jaate hain.

Correct ich treatment ki har cheez isi se follow hoti hai. Aap repeatedly dose karte hain, kayi din tak,
kyunki aap parasites ki har wave ke us ek vulnerable stage tak pahunchne ka wait kar rahe hain. Spots ka gayab
hona ye matlab hai ki trophonts reproduce karne ke liye drop off ho gaye hain — ye cycle ka midpoint hai, iska
end nahi. Poora cycle garam paani mein roughly 48 ghante leta hai aur 15°C ke paas ek hafte ya usse zyada tak
stretch ho jaata hai, isliye treatment intervals temperature-dependent hote hain: roughly tropical temperature
par 2-3 din ke antar se 3 doses, cold water mein 3-5 din ke antar se 5 doses.

Temperature badhane se cycle compress hota hai aur aap jaldi vulnerable stage tak pahunchte hain. Ye khud
parasite ko nahi maarta, aur sources iske help karne par sharply disagree karte hain. Ye dissolved oxygen bhi
kam karta hai exactly us waqt jab infected gills wala fish ise sabse kam afford kar sakta hai. Ise ek real
medication ke saath accelerator ke taur par use kariye, sirf un tanks mein jahan har inhabitant temperature
tolerate kar sake, extra aeration chalate hue.

Tank infected hai, fish nahi. Poore tank ko treat kariye, poore course ke liye.
