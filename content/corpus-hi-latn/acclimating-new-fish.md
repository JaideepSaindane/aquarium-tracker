immediate_actions:
  - Bag ko seal karke tank mein 15-20 minute float kariye taaki temperature gradually equalise ho jaaye, phir hi bag kholiye.
  - Bag mein thoda-thoda tank ka paani mix kariye, 20-30+ minutes mein (sensitive species ke liye drip acclimation, ya simpler approach ke liye baar-baar thodi matra add karna) - fish ko directly alag water chemistry mein mat chhodiye.
  - Bag/transport ka original paani kabhi bhi display tank mein mat daaliye - isme store ya pichhli tank se pathogens ya stress hormones ho sakte hain; fish ko net se nikaal kar bag ka paani discard kar dijiye.
  - Fish release karne ke turant baad tank ki light off rakhiye aur zyada handling/observation avoid kariye, taaki naye fish ko settle hone ka time mile bina extra stress ke.

do_not_do:

  - Acclimation skip mat kariye sirf isliye ki fish bag mein "theek" lag raha hai - fast chemistry shift se internal stress hamesha short term mein visible nahi hota.

Acclimation ek narrower lekin real risk address karta hai: fish jis paani mein transport hua tha aur jis paani mein use release kiya ja raha hai, dono ke chemistry mein sudden, bada difference. Temperature usually zyada familiar concern hai (sealed bag ko float karna), lekin paani ki chemistry - pH, hardness, dissolved compounds - bhi store ke system aur ghar ki tank ke beech utni hi meaningfully different ho sakti hai, isiliye time ke saath tank ka paani bag mein gradually mix karna, sirf float karne se zyada complete process hai.

Bag ka paani khud kabhi bhi display tank mein nahi jaana chahiye, acclimation process se independent - ye ek shared retail system ya doosri tank ka transport paani hai aur isme real, agar quantify karna mushkil bhi ho, pathogen aur stress-hormone risk hota hai. Fish ko net se nikaal kar bag ka paani discard karna ek simple habit hai jo kuch unwanted introduce hone ke chances ko meaningfully kam karta hai.

Acclimation aur quarantine (corpus:quarantine-setup) ko aksar ek hi samjha jaata hai lekin ye alag risks address karte hain - acclimation pehle ghante ke acute chemistry/temperature shock ko avoid karne ke baare mein hai, jabki quarantine iske baare mein hai ki naye laaye gaye fish mein incubate ho rahi kisi bhi disease ko dikhne ka time mile, isse pehle ki wo established display tank aur uske existing stock tak pahunche. Ek careful keeper dono karta hai, ek ki jagah dusra nahi.
