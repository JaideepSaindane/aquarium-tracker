Cycle crash tab hota hai jab ek established, mature biofilter damage ya bilkul mar jaata hai, aur tank effectively original cycling process ka ek mini version dobara experience karti hai - ammonia aur nitrite ek tank mein spike kar sakte hain jo mahine ya saalon se stable thi, keepers ko off guard catch karte hue kyunki "tank already cycled hai" ko ek permanent, ek-baar-set fact ki tarah treat kiya jaata hai, na ki ek ongoing biological population jo damage ho sakti hai.

Sabse common real-world causes sab maintenance mistakes hain: filter media ko removed tank water ki jagah chlorinated tap ke neeche rinse karna, saare filter media ko staggered karne ki jagah ek saath replace karna, ek medication course (especially antibiotics) jo beneficial nitrifying bacteria ko us pathogen se distinguish nahi karta jise treat karna tha, aur extended power cuts - India mein bahut common - jo filter flow ko itna der rok dete hain ki media anoxic ho jaaye aur aerobic bacterial colony die back ho jaaye.

Ek baar crash hone ke baad, ise exactly ek acute ammonia ya nitrite spike jaise treat kariye (corpus:ammonia-spike, corpus:nitrite-spike) aur daily testing resume kariye jab tak biofilter genuinely recover na ho jaaye. Doosri healthy, established tank ke media se seed karna wapas aane ka fastest tareeka hai, same reason se jo kisi bhi cycle ko speed up karta hai.
