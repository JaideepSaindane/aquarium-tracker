Velvet, jo dinoflagellate parasite *Piscinoodinium* se hota hai, aksar ich se confuse ho jaata hai kyunki dono skin par ek fine coating ke roop mein present hote hain, lekin velvet kaafi fast progress karta hai aur gills ko zyada aggressively attack karta hai — ek fish first visible signs se critical respiratory distress tak ek-do din mein pahunch sakti hai. Coating khud ich ke distinct raised white grains se finer aur dustier hoti hai, aksar ek gold, rust ya yellow-brown tint ke saath jo ek dim room mein low angle par torch se sabse aasaani se dikhta hai.

Is parasite ki biology ki ek unusual aur genuinely useful feature yeh hai ki iska free-living stage partly photosynthesis par depend karta hai, isliye light exposure kam karna — tank ya room ko darken karna — measurably uska lifecycle slow karta hai. Isme koi cost nahi hai aur koi risk nahi hai, aur medication ke saath turant karne layak hai, afterthought ke roop mein nahi.

Velvet kitna fast move karta hai iski wajah se, "ek din observe karo aur dekho worse ho raha hai ya nahi" wala approach jo milder issues ke liye reasonable caution hai, yahan actively risky hai. Gill damage particularly ek point tak progress kar sakta hai jahan successful parasite elimination bhi ek badly affected fish ko nahi bacha pata, isi liye prompt treatment aur, bad cases mein, specific tank par experienced guidance slower diseases ke muqable zyada matter karta hai.
