Ek heater jo off ho kar fail ho gaya hai, stuck-on heater se slower-moving problem hai, lekin tropical tank
ke liye still genuinely urgent hai, kyunki kayi popular tropical species ka comfortable temperature range
fairly narrow hota hai aur sustained cold ke ek din mein hi measurably decline shuru ho jaata hai. Sabse pehle
check hamesha power source hi honi chahiye — ek unplugged heater, ek tripped breaker, ya power cut
(corpus:power-cuts) — ye sab genuinely failed unit se zyada common aur cheaper explanations hain, aur inhe
pehle rule out karne se ek unnecessary replacement kharidne se bacha ja sakta hai.

Agar heater genuinely fail ho gaya hai, to working heater lagne ke baad bhi temperature ko gradually restore
kiya jaana chahiye — jo reasoning overheated tank ko slowly cool karne mein apply hoti hai, wahi yahan reverse
mein apply hoti hai. Target temperature par sudden jump khud ek stressor hai, cold exposure ke upar jo already
ho chuka hai.

Ek spare heater rakhna, ya kam se kam yeh jaanna ki kahan se quickly source kiya ja sake, genuinely
temperature-sensitive species keeping walon ke liye ek reasonable precaution hai, dekhte hue ki routine power
interruptions aur equipment failures dono kitne common hain.
