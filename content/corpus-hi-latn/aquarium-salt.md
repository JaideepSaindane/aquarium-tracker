Plain sodium chloride (non-iodised aquarium, kosher, ya pickling salt - iodised table salt kabhi nahi) specific situations mein genuinely useful tool hai, sabse notably nitrite poisoning ke emergency treatment ke roop mein (dekhiye corpus:nitrite-spike, jahan chloride ions gill par nitrite uptake ko competitively block karte hain). Yahi wo jagah bhi hai jahan freshwater fishkeeping ka single sabse common aur sabse dangerous fatal belief rehta hai: "salt natural hai, toh hamesha safe hai."

Ye belief common freshwater community fish ke bade hisse ke liye sahi nahi hai. Bahut se catfish aur loaches (generally scaleless species), zyadatar soft-water tetras, freshwater shrimp, zyadatar snails, live plants, aur electroreceptive species jaise elephant nose aur black ghost knife fish salt ko poorly se very poorly tolerate karte hain, aur jo dose hardy livebearer community ke liye routine hai wo inme se kisi ke liye bhi same tank mein harmful ya fatal ho sakti hai.

Correct default "salt generally fish ke liye good hai" nahi hai - ye hai "salt ke specific, legitimate uses hain, aur har use se pehle tank ka actual stocking pehle check hona chahiye." Ek mixed community tank ko salt add karne se pehle uske sabse sensitive resident ke against evaluate karna chahiye, sabse hardy resident ke against nahi.
