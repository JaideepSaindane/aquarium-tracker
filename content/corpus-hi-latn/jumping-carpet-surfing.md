Jumping — ek fish ka paani se bahar nikal jaana aur tank se bahar khatam hona, aksar bachaane ke liye bahut
der se mila — poori tarah preventable hai, phir bhi keepers ke liye ek common, painful loss bana rehta hai jo
ye realise nahi kar paate ki ek specific species ko secure lid chahiye tha, ya jo maan lete hain ki unka
existing lid actually gap-free hai.

Zyada tar real jumping incidents ki practical reality ye hai ki tank mein lid thi hi — fish ek equipment
cutout, cord entry point, ya corner seam ke aas paas ek gap se escape hua, poori tarah open top se nahi.
Yehi wajah hai ki "kya mere tank mein lid hai" galat sawal hai; sahi sawal ye hai ki kya us lid ka har gap
tank ke specific fish ko actually rokne ke liye kaafi chhota hai.

Hobby mein kuch groups strong jumping tendency ke liye well known hain — particularly kayi killifish aur
hatchetfish, saath mein kuch tetras aur danios — aur in species ke liye ek secure, gap-free lid stocking se
pehle research aur confirm karne ki ek hard requirement hai, ek optional nicety nahi. Normally calm species
bhi ek sudden fright se startle hone par jump kar sakti hain, isliye ek secure lid specific species se
independent ek good general practice hai, aur water level ko rim se sensible distance neeche rakhna kisi bhi
jump-prone cheez ke liye risk aur kam kar deta hai.
