  - Naye fish ghar laane se pehle, jab bhi practical ho, ek separate, simple quarantine tank set up kariye (bare-bottom ya minimal decor, easy observation aur cleaning ke liye, fish ke hisaab se appropriately sized), problem aane ke baad nahi.

Quarantine ka purpose hai "purchase ke time healthy dikhna" aur "actually kisi bhi transmissible cheez se free hona" ke beech ke gap ko close karna — is corpus mein kahin aur cover ki gayi bahut saari diseases (ich, flukes, internal parasites, kayi bacterial infections) ka ek incubation period hota hai jiske dauraan ek infected fish koi bhi visible sign nahi dikhati. Ek separate holding tank, simple aur observe/clean karne mein easy rakha gaya, us incubation window ko pass hone ka time deta hai isse pehle ki naya fish ek established display tank aur uska existing, otherwise healthy stock tak pahunche.

Practical setup concept jitna hi matter karta hai: ek quarantine tank jo khud uncycled hai, ek second stressor (ammonia/nitrite exposure) introduce karta hai us cheez ke upar jise naya fish already transport ke baad adjust kar raha hai — isi liye ek spare, pre-seeded filter (jaise main display tank ke saath cycle kiya gaya ek sponge filter) rakhna specifically quarantine use ke liye experienced keepers mein ek common aur effective habit hai.

Shared equipment ke through cross-contamination quarantine ke poore purpose ko accidentally defeat karne ka ek aasaan tarika hai — quarantine tank mein use hua ek net ya siphon, phir bina disinfect kiye display tank mein use hone se exactly wahi pathogens transfer ho sakte hain jinhe catch karna quarantine ka matlab tha. Dedicated equipment, ya uses ke beech ek simple disinfection step, is gap ko close kar deta hai.
