Levamisole aur fenbendazole dono effective, nematode-specific dewormers hain jo internal roundworm infections
jaise Camallanus aur Capillaria ke liye use hote hain (dekhiye corpus:internal-worms), lekin dono hamesha ek
dedicated aquarium formulation mein label par clear aquarium dose ke saath nahi bikte — dono widely livestock
ya veterinary products (cattle, poultry, ya doosre animals ke liye) ke roop mein available hain, ek poori
tarah different context aur body of water ke liye dosed.

Ye gap in medications ka single sabse bada real-world risk hai: koi keeper livestock dewormer source karke
bina verified conversion ke aquarium dose estimate kar raha ho, ye hobby mein ek well-documented aur serious
overdose route hai. Correct use ka matlab hai ya to genuinely aquarium-formulated product apni khud ki clear
dosing ke saath dhoondhna, ya carefully ek verified, aquarium-specific conversion guide follow karna, guess
karne ke bajaye.

Fenbendazole ek additional, specific caution carry karta hai dosing se aage: ye snails aur kuch doosri
invertebrate life ke liye toxic hai un doses par bhi jo otherwise fish treat karne ke liye appropriate hain,
isliye keeping-worthy snail populations wale tanks ko ya to ek alag medication choice (levamisole, jo
broadly non-target invertebrates par gentler maana jaata hai) chahiye ya treatment se pehle snails ko
carefully remove karna chahiye.
