Copper kai external parasites ke against ek zyada effective treatments mein se ek hai, velvet included, lekin ye is corpus mein invertebrates ke liye sabse least forgiving medications mein se bhi ek hai - shrimp, snails aur doosre freshwater invertebrates ko fish treat karne ke liye zaroori concentration par essentially koi meaningful tolerance nahi hoti, jisse "bas dose mein thoda careful raho" ek viable safety strategy nahi ban paati, jaise wo shayad kisi zyada forgiving medication ke liye ho sakti hai.

Do practical consequences follow karte hain. Pehla, copper treatment kisi bhi invertebrates se alag hospital tank mein honi chahiye, ya unhe fully remove karne ke baad - koi safe compromise dose nahi hai jo same paani mein fish treat kare aur shrimp ya snails ko bacha le. Dusra, copper substrate, decor, aur filter media mein bind ho jaata hai, aur active treatment khatam hone ke baad bhi lambe time tak - weeks se months - leach hota reh sakta hai, matlab ek baar copper se treat ki gayi display tank specific remediation aur ye confirm karne wali testing ke bina, ki wo actually clear ho gaya hai, dobara invertebrates se genuinely safely restock nahi ho sakti.

Required copper concentration bhi water hardness ke saath vary karti hai, isiliye sirf fixed recipe se dose karna unreliable hai - copper test kit, jo actual therapeutic level confirm aur maintain karne ke liye use hota hai, is medication ko sahi tarike se use karne ka ek zaroori hissa hai, koi optional extra nahi.
