Bacterial ulcers aur haemorrhagic septicaemia ek serious, systemic bacterial infection describe karte hain - open sores, skin ke neeche red streaking (especially fin bases aur belly ke around), ya diffuse reddening ke roop mein visible - jo simple fin rot jaisi localised problem se ek step aage hai. Ye ek whole-body infection hai, aur ise waise hi recognise aur treat karne ki zaroorat hai, milder conditions ke liye appropriate zyada cautious, water-quality-first sequence se nahi.

Iss entry ka practical purpose ye galti prevent karna hai: visible red streaking ya open sores ko sirf external dip ya topical spot treatment se treat karna. Systemic infection ke liye systemic treatment approach chahiye - internal antibiotics, aksar medicated food ke through kyunki infection surface tak confined nahi hai, ya genuinely systemic water-column antibacterial. Ye dekhne ke liye wait karna ki milder, external-only treatment pehle kaam karta hai ya nahi, us time ko waste karta hai jo ye condition generously nahi deti.

Jaise iss corpus mein zyadatar bacterial disease mein hota hai, chronic poor water quality sabse common underlying contributor hai, jo fish ki defences ko itna weaken karti hai ki opportunistic infection systemic spread kar le - prompt medical treatment ke saath water quality correct karna immediate infection aur wo conditions dono address karta hai jinki wajah se ye itna severe hua.
