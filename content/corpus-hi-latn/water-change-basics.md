immediate_actions:
  - Stability ke liye bade infrequent water changes ke jagah routine partial water changes kariye (commonly 20-30% weekly zyadatar community tanks ke liye, stocking level aur nitrate readings ke hisaab se adjust kiya hua).
  - Tap water ko tank ko touch karne se pehle hamesha ek chlorine aur chloramine dono ke liye rated dechlorinator/water conditioner se treat kariye (sirf chlorine wala nahi — bahut saari municipal supplies chloramine use karti hain, jisko specifically usi ke liye rated conditioner chahiye).
  - Naye water aur tank water ke temperature ko reasonably close match kariye add karne se pehle, ek avoidable temperature shock se bachne ke liye.
  - Kisi tank mein pH-ammonia interaction check kariye (corpus:ph-ammonia-trap) kisi bhi water change se pehle jisme low pH aur elevated ammonia dono hon — us specific combination mein, standard water-change advice helpful ke jagah dangerous ban sakti hai.
  - Water changes ke dauraan substrate debris vacuum kariye routine maintenance ke roop mein, kyunki accumulated waste changes ke beech ammonia/nitrate load ka ek slow-building source hai.
do_not_do:
  - Untreated tap water ko directly tank mein mat daaliye — chlorine aur chloramine dono fish aur biofilter ke liye toxic hain.
  - Ek bahut bada water change regular chote wale ka substitute banakar mat kariye — bade sudden parameter ya temperature swings khud ek stressor hain, aur ek neglected tank par ek bada change especially un fish ko shock karne ka risk rakhta hai jo slowly degraded conditions ke adapt ho chuki hain.
  - Water change se pehle pH-ammonia interaction check karna skip mat kariye jab pH low ho aur ammonia present ho — dekhiye corpus:ph-ammonia-trap ki yeh specific combination normal water-change advice ka exception kyun hai.

Regular partial water changes hobby ki sabse effective routine maintenance habit hain — yeh accumulating nitrate aur doosre dissolved waste products ko dilute karte hain jinhe biological filtration remove nahi karti, aur yeh infrequent, bade changes se tank ki stability ke liye kaafi gentle hain. Ek steady 20-30% weekly rhythm, ek heavily stocked tank ke liye upar aur ek lightly stocked, heavily planted tank ke liye neeche adjust kiya hua, kisi bhi fixed universal number se zyadatar community setups ke liye better suit karta hai.

Tank ko touch karne se pehle tap water ko properly treat karna non-negotiable hai — municipal water supplies commonly chlorine ya chloramine carry karti hain specifically kyunki yeh bacteria maarne mein effective hain, jo exactly wahi wajah hai ki yeh fish aur tank ki apni beneficial bacterial colony ko harm karne mein bhi equally effective hain. Ek conditioner jo specifically chloramine ke liye rated ho (sirf chlorine nahi) important hai, kyunki simple dechlorinators chloramine ke ammonia component ko neutralise nahi karte.

"Regular water changes hamesha safe default hain" ka ek genuine exception pH-ammonia trap (corpus:ph-ammonia-trap) hai — low pH aur elevated ammonia dono wale tank mein, ek normal water change relatively harmless ionised ammonia ko iske toxic form mein convert kar sakta hai. Water change se pehle is specific combination ko check karna ek chota habit hai jo ek real, chahe uncommon hi sahi, risk avoid karta hai.
