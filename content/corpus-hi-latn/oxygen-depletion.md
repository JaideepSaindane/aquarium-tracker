Oxygen depletion is corpus ke almost kisi bhi cheez se zyada fast maarta hai, aur iska defining symptom pattern — multiple ya saari fish ka surface par gathered hoke ek saath gasping karna — usually us simultaneity se hi kisi individual fish ke health problem se distinguish ho jaata hai. Ek akeli fish akele gasping karna zyada aksar us fish ke kisi specific cheez ki taraf point karta hai (gill parasite, internal issue); poore tank ka ek saath gasping karna water ke khud oxygen mein short pad jaane ki taraf point karta hai.

Kayi causes independently nahi, balki ek doosre ke saath compound hote hain: heat water ki oxygen hold karne ki capacity kam karta hai aur saath mein fish ki oxygen demand bhi badhata hai (dekhiye corpus:heatwave-overheating); overstocking fixed supply ke against total demand badhata hai; ruka hua filter ya air pump surface agitation ka main source hata deta hai jo oxygen exchange drive karta hai; aur planted tanks mein, oxygen naturally dawn se pehle ke hours mein sabse kam hota hai, kyunki plants raat ko photosynthesis ki absence mein oxygen consume karte hain — yeh pattern aur bhi worse ho jaata hai agar CO2 injection bina nighttime shutoff ke chalta rahe.

Kyunki immediate fix — surface agitation aur aeration badhaana — safe, fast, aur underlying cause chahe kuch bhi ho helpful hai, oxygen depletion suspect hote hi yeh first response hona chahiye, underlying cause ka diagnosis pehle nahi, parallel mein hona chahiye.
