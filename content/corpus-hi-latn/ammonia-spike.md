symptoms:
  - Surface par gasping ya filter outflow ke paas hang karna
  - Red ya inflamed gills; bad cases mein gills burnt ya ragged lagti hain
  - Lethargy, bottom par baithna, khana refuse karna
  - Clamped fins, darkened colour, body ya fins par red streaks
  - Acute poisoning mein - spinning, disorientation, convulsions
  - Pichhle 6-8 hafton mein set up hui tank mein deaths
likely_causes:
  - Tank ko biofilter ki zaroorat se kam time cycle kiya gaya (typically establish hone mein 4-8 hafte lagte hain)
  - Ek saath bahut zyada fish add karna, ya setup ke bahut jaldi baad add karna
  - Overfeeding, ya kahin nazar na aa raha uneaten food/dead fish decompose ho raha
  - Filter media chlorinated tap water mein rinse kiya gaya, ya poora replace kiya gaya, jisse bacteria mar gaya
  - Filter kai ghanton (power cut) ke liye ruk gaya aur media anoxic ho gaya
  - Antibiotic ya medication course jisne nitrifying bacteria wipe out kar diya
  - Tap water mein chloramine jise ek dechlorinator ke saath dose kiya gaya jo ammonia byproduct ko bind nahi karta
immediate_actions:
  - Abhi ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, pH aur temperature test kariye. Bina pH aur temperature ke aap severity judge nahi kar sakte - same total ammonia reading pH 8.2 par pH 6.8 se kahin zyada toxic hoti hai, aur warm mein cool se zyada toxic.
  - Feeding poori tarah band kariye. Fish kai dinon tak bina khaaye safe rehti hain; add ki gayi har gram food aur ammonia hai.
  - Water change size choose karne se pehle pH check kariye. Agar pH 7.0 ya usse upar hai, temperature-matched, fully dechlorinated water se 50% ya usse zyada ka water change kariye - ye ek hi action hai jo reliably ammonia remove karta hai, mask nahi karta. Agar pH 7.0 se neeche HAI AUR total ammonia 0.5 ppm ya usse zyada hai, higher-pH water se ek bada change mat kariye; iski jagah pH-matched water ke saath kai chhote 20-25% changes kariye (dekhiye corpus:ph-ammonia-trap). Agar pH 7.0 se neeche HAI AUR total ammonia 5 ppm ya usse zyada hai, escalate kariye: fish ko already-cycled, parameter-matched water mein move karna in-place water change se safer hai.
  - Full tank volume ke liye ek dechlorinator dose kariye jo ammonia bhi bind karta ho (Seachem Prime aur equivalents) label rate par. Ye free ammonia ko roughly 24-48 hours ke liye ek less toxic form mein convert karta hai; ye time deta hai, cycle fix nahi karta.
  - Aeration badhaiye. Ammonia-damaged gills efficiently oxygen extract nahi kar sakte, isliye surface agitation badhaiye ya airstone add kariye.
  - 4-6 ghante baad re-test kariye aur agar ammonia abhi bhi total 0.25 ppm se upar hai to water change repeat kariye. Daily repeat kariye jab tak biofilter catch up na ho jaaye.
  - Jab tak ammonia aur nitrite dono poore hafte 0 na padhein, koi aur fish add mat kariye.
do_not_do:
  - Jab pH 7.0 se neeche HAI AUR total ammonia 0.5 ppm ya usse zyada hai, higher-pH water se ek single large water change mat kariye. pH badhana stored ammonium (NH4+) ko free ammonia (NH3) mein almost instantly convert kar deta hai aur surviving fish ko maar sakta hai. Iski jagah pH-matched water ke saath kai chhote 20-25% changes kariye, aur user ko batayein ki aap slow tarika kyun use kar rahe hain - "bas aur paani change karo" wali instinct hi yahan tank ko marti hai. (Dekhiye corpus:ph-ammonia-trap. 5 ppm total ammonia se upar ye escalate hota hai - dekhiye when_to_escalate.)
  - Kisi bhi medication se treat mat kariye. Ammonia poisoning ek infection nahi hai. Antibiotics jo bhi biofilter bacha hai use khatam kar denge aur spike ko permanent bana denge.
  - Filter media clean ya replace mat kariye. Wahi aapke nitrifying bacteria rehte hain. Agar genuinely clogged hai, tank se nikale gaye paani mein gently rinse kariye - tap ke neeche kabhi nahi.
  - Ammonia-binding dechlorinator akele ko treatment maan kar rely mat kariye. Ye roughly 24-48 ghanton ka ek holding measure hai, iske detoxifying claims debated hain, aur ye biofilter build karne mein kuch nahi karta.
  - Total-ammonia test result ko toxicity result jaisa mat treat kariye. Number ko pH aur temperature ke saath report kariye warna advice guesswork hai.
  - Water change aur dechlorinator ke saath ek hi samay pH-up, buffers, ya "ammonia remover" chemicals mat add kariye. Changes stack karna tank ko us direction mein unstable bana deta hai jo aap predict nahi kar sakte.
  - Ek established lekin neglected tank par 100% water change mat kariye (dekhiye Old Tank Syndrome, id old-tank-syndrome) - chronically poor water ke adapted fish osmotic shock mein chali jaati hain.
  - Kisi bhi disease history wali tank se seeded sponge ya media use mat kariye. Aap bacteria ke saath disease bhi transfer kar denge.
when_to_escalate:
  - pH 7.0 se neeche AUR total ammonia 5 ppm ya usse zyada - fish ko already-cycled, parameter-matched water mein move karna in-place water change se safer hai (Merck). Water-change karne ki jagah escalate kariye.
  - Fish spinning, convulsing, ya side par lying - acute neurological toxicity, minutes matter karte hain.
  - Kai large water changes ke baad bhi ammonia elevated rehti hai - ek decomposing source dhundiye (rocks ke peeche dead fish, dead snail, buried food) ya supply mein chloramine.
  - Tap water khud ammonia ke liye positive test karta hai - jahan chloramine use hota hai wahan common hai; ek alag dechlorinator aur before/after confirmation testing chahiye.

Ek test kit total ammonia nitrogen (TAN) report karta hai, jo do cheezon ka sum hai: ammonium (NH4+), jo comparatively harmless hai, aur un-ionised ammonia (NH3), jo roughly sau guna zyada toxic hai. Total ko in dono ke beech split kya karta hai - ye hai pH aur temperature. Higher pH aur higher temperature balance ko NH3 ki taraf push karte hain. Isiliye ek soft, acidic, 24°C tank mein 1 ppm total ammonia ek manageable problem hai jabki ek hard, alkaline, 30°C African cichlid tank mein 1 ppm ek emergency hai - aur isiliye sirf ammonia number se di gayi koi bhi advice unreliable hai.

Reference points: un-ionised ammonia 0.05 mg/L se neeche generally non-harmful mana jaata hai, gill damage roughly usi level ke around start hota hai, aur 2 mg/L bahut si species ke liye lethal hai. Total ammonia par, freshwater fish ko 1 mg/L se neeche rehna chahiye, practical aquarium target zero ke saath. (AquaAI freshwater-only hai; marine figures deliberately yahan carry nahi ki gayi hain.)

New tank syndrome wahi problem hai ek known cause ke saath: nitrifying bacteria jo ammonia ko nitrite mein aur nitrite ko nitrate mein convert karte hain establish hone mein roughly 4-8 hafte lete hain, aur usse pehle add ki gayi fish apne hi waste mein reh rahi hoti hai. Koi bhi product nahi hai jo ye requirement remove kare. Water changes aur na khilana hi hai jo fish ko iske through le jaate hain.

Ek trap ko naam dena zaroori hai, aur iske do stages hain. Ek aisi tank mein jahan pH gir gaya hai aur ammonia accumulate ho gaya hai, zyadatar ammonia relatively safe NH4+ ke roop mein baitha hota hai. Ek badi volume higher-pH tap water ki dump karna ise minutes mein NH3 mein flip kar deta hai.

- **pH 7.0 se neeche AUR total ammonia 0.5 ppm ya usse zyada** - higher-pH water se ek single large water change mat kariye. pH-matched water ke saath kai chhote 20-25% changes kariye, aur user ko samjhaiye kyun: har chhota change ammonia remove karta hai bina pH ko itna door move kiye ki jo bacha hai wo toxic free form mein convert ho jaaye.
- **pH 7.0 se neeche AUR total ammonia 5 ppm ya usse zyada** - escalate kariye. Fish ko already-cycled, parameter-matched water mein move karna in-place water change se safer hai (Merck).

Ye do numbers, 0.5 ppm aur 5 ppm, product mein kahin bhi is trap ke liye ek hi thresholds hain. Poori entry `corpus:ph-ammonia-trap` hai.
