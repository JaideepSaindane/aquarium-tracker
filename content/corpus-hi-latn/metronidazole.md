Metronidazole internal protozoal parasites ke liye standard treatment hai — sabse zyada Hexamita, jo
hole-in-the-head disease se relevant hai (corpus:hexamita-hole-in-the-head) — aur iski effectiveness delivery
method par heavily depend karti hai, ek aise tarike se jise galat karna easy hai. Kyunki target parasite
internally, gut mein rehta hai, sirf medication ko tank water mein dissolve karke ye umeed rakhna ki fish
apne gills ya skin se enough absorb kar lega, medicated food ke through directly dene se kaafi kam reliable
hai, jahan fish actually ek controlled, effective dose ingest karta hai.

Ye practice mein medication use hone ka ek common aur consequential gap hai: koi keeper water column dose
karta hai, koi improvement nahi dikhta, aur wo conclude karta hai ki medication kaam nahi karti ya diagnosis
galat thi, jabki zyada likely explanation ye hai ki water dosing akele iss specific condition ke liye kabhi
sabse effective delivery route thi hi nahi.

Jab bhi fish abhi bhi kha raha ho, tab medicated food preferred method hai. Water-column dosing ek reasonable
fallback bani rehti hai un fish ke liye jinhone khana band kar diya hai, honestly samjha jaana chahiye ki ye
is specific use case ke liye ek kam reliable option hai, ek equally good alternative nahi.
