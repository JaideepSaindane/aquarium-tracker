Malachite green is an effective, well-established treatment against ich and several other external
parasites, frequently sold in combination with formalin in commercial "ich treatment" products. It carries
two genuine, well-supported cautions: it is teratogenic, meaning it can harm developing fish eggs, so it
should never be used in an active breeding setup with eggs present, and it can stain silicone and decor.

A blanket claim sometimes repeated in the hobby — that malachite green is categorically unsafe for all
scaleless fish or all invertebrates — is not well supported by the evidence and is worth correcting
directly, since it has pushed some keepers toward alternatives (aquarium salt, copper-based treatments)
that are actually more dangerous for the exact species (many scaleless fish, most invertebrates) the
avoidance was meant to protect. The responsible position is to dose strictly to label instructions and
check the specific product and species combination, not to avoid the medication category entirely based on
an overly broad rule.
