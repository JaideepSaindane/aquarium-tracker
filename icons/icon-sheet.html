Usage

  • Nav icons use currentColor: #65747A inactive, #168A8A active with a #DDF1EE pill.
  • Keep stroke at 1.75 when scaling to 20–28px; the tank-type tile colours are the only place colour-coding appears.
  • Export the primary app icon at 1024×1024 without the rounded corners for iOS (the OS applies its own mask).
